# Day 30 – Supply Chain Strategy Simulator

## Objective

Build and evaluate an AI-powered Supply Chain Strategy Simulator that demonstrates how different supply chain decisions affect business performance, operational efficiency, costs, inventory management, transportation, and overall supply chain resilience.

---

## Task Performed

Today I used Claude AI to generate an interactive HTML application named **Supply Chain Strategy Simulator**.

The workflow included:

1. Reading the provided resources.
2. Watching the solution video.
3. Opening Claude AI.
4. Setting Claude effort level to Low.
5. Starting a new conversation.
6. Using the provided Supply Chain Strategy Simulator prompt.
7. Generating the complete HTML application.
8. Saving the generated HTML file.
9. Opening the simulator in a web browser.
10. Reading the beginner-friendly introduction.
11. Reviewing the randomly generated company profile.
12. Choosing a supplier strategy.
13. Selecting a factory location.
14. Choosing a warehouse strategy.
15. Selecting a transportation method.
16. Choosing an inventory strategy.
17. Observing how every decision affected business metrics.
18. Reviewing the final optimization dashboard.
19. Analyzing the Overall Supply Chain Score.
20. Reviewing strengths, weaknesses, biggest risks, and recommended improvements.
21. Replaying the simulator using different randomly generated companies.
22. Taking screenshots of the results.

## Key Learnings

- Every supply chain decision affects cost, speed, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.
- Supplier diversification reduces operational risk.
- Factory location influences logistics costs and delivery times.
- Transportation methods involve trade-offs between speed and expense.
- Inventory strategies impact storage costs and product availability.
- Interactive simulations help understand real-world business decision-making.

## My Reflection

This project provided practical insight into supply chain optimization and strategic planning. By experimenting with different business scenarios, I learned how AI-powered simulations can help organizations evaluate operational decisions, reduce risks, and improve overall performance.
