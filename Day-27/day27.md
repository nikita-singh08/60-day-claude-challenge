# Day 27 – Prior Authorization Story Simulator

## Objective

Build and test an interactive Prior Authorization Story Simulator that explains the healthcare prior authorization process through a story-driven experience with branching choices and educational insights.

---

# Task Performed

Today I used Claude AI to generate an interactive Prior Authorization Story Simulator.

The workflow included:

1. Continuing the conversation from Day 26.
2. Using the provided Prior Authorization Story Simulator prompt.
3. Generating the complete HTML application.
4. Saving the generated HTML file.
5. Opening the application in a web browser.
6. Starting the interactive healthcare story.
7. Following Rahul's healthcare journey.
8. Reading Priya's explanations at every stage.
9. Selecting one of the available choices after each chapter.
10. Progressing through all eight story chapters.
11. Monitoring the progress bar throughout the simulation.
12. Completing the prior authorization approval process.
13. Reviewing the final learning summary.
14. Restarting the story to explore alternative dialogue paths.
15. Taking screenshots of important scenes and outcomes.

- Storytelling makes complex healthcare workflows easier to understand.
- Interactive decisions improve engagement and retention.
