# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Simulator

## Objective

Build and test an AI-powered Supply Chain Crisis Simulator that models business disruptions, supplier negotiations, executive decision-making, and AI-driven investment strategies through an interactive React-based application.

---

# Task Performed

Today I used Claude AI to generate an interactive React HTML application called **Operation Lifeline**.

The workflow included:

1. Starting a new conversation in Claude.
2. Using the provided Operation Lifeline prompt.
3. Generating the complete React HTML application.
4. Saving the generated HTML file.
5. Opening the simulator in a web browser.
6. Starting the simulation.
7. Reviewing the randomly generated company profile.
8. Analyzing the assigned supply chain crisis.
9. Selecting three crisis response actions.
10. Completing supplier negotiation rounds.
11. Answering CEO leadership questions.
12. Selecting two AI-powered supply chain investments.
13. Reviewing the executive dashboard and business performance scores.
14. Replaying the simulation using different crisis scenarios.
15. Taking screenshots of the simulator and results.

# Key Learnings

- Supply chain resilience depends on proactive planning and rapid decision-making.
- Supplier negotiations directly affect operational continuity.
- AI investments can improve forecasting, inventory management, and logistics.
- Executive dashboards help evaluate strategic decisions using measurable business metrics.
- Scenario-based simulations improve understanding of crisis management.

# My Reflection

This project demonstrated how AI can simulate real-world supply chain disruptions and executive decision-making. The simulator provided valuable insight into crisis response, supplier management, business strategy, and the impact of AI technologies on operational resilience.
