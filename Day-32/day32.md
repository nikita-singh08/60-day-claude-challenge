# Day 32 – Marketing Strategy Simulator

## Objective

Build and test an AI-powered Marketing Strategy Simulator that helps develop marketing strategies, select suitable platforms, create content plans, respond to marketing events, and evaluate business growth.

## Task Performed

Today I used Claude AI to generate an interactive HTML application called **Marketing Strategy Simulator**.

The workflow included:

1. Starting a new conversation in Claude.
2. Setting Claude effort level to Low.
3. Using the provided Marketing Strategy Simulator prompt.
4. Generating the complete HTML application.
5. Saving the generated HTML file.
6. Opening the application in a web browser.
7. Choosing Business, Personal Brand, or Random Client mode.
8. Understanding the target audience.
9. Selecting the best marketing platforms.
10. Choosing three content pillars.
11. Generating the 30-day marketing roadmap.
12. Responding to the randomized marketing event.
13. Reviewing the final Growth Report.
14. Studying the reusable Claude prompts provided after each section.
15. Replaying the simulator using different businesses or personal brands.
16. Taking screenshots of the results.

## Key Learnings

- A successful marketing strategy starts with understanding the target audience.
- Different businesses and personal brands require different marketing platforms.
- Content pillars help maintain consistency and direction in content creation.
- A structured 30-day roadmap makes marketing activities easier to execute.
- Marketing strategies need to adapt when unexpected events occur.
- AI can help generate, organize, and evaluate marketing strategies quickly.
- Replaying different scenarios helps understand how strategic decisions affect growth.

## My Reflection

This task helped me understand how AI can support marketing strategy development. The simulator demonstrated how audience analysis, platform selection, content planning, and adaptability can influence marketing performance.

I also learned how reusable AI prompts can make repetitive marketing analysis faster and more systematic.
