# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Objective

Generate a Customer & MVP Blueprint using Claude AI to identify the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), customer personas, pain points, customer journey, MVP features, pricing strategy, and founder action plan for a startup idea.

---

# Task Performed

Today I used Claude AI to generate a Customer & MVP Blueprint based on the Startup Validation Report from Day 22.

The workflow included:

1. Continuing the conversation from Day 22.
2. Uploading the Startup Validation Report.
3. Using the provided Customer & MVP Blueprint prompt.
4. Generating the complete blueprint.
5. Reviewing the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
6. Analyzing the Buyer Persona.
7. Reviewing the Top 10 Customer Pain Points.
8. Studying the Customer Journey Map.
9. Reviewing Buying Triggers and Customer Objections.
10. Analyzing the MVP Recommendation.
11. Reviewing MoSCoW Prioritization.
12. Studying the Pricing Hypothesis.
13. Reviewing the Top 5 Business Risks.
14. Reviewing the Founder Action Sheet.
15. Saving the generated report.
16. Taking screenshots of important sections.

# Key Learnings

- Understanding the target customer is essential before building a product.
- Pain points help prioritize valuable features.
- MoSCoW prioritization separates essential features from optional ones.
- Customer journey mapping improves product design.
- A pricing hypothesis provides an initial monetization strategy.
- Founder action plans help organize the next development steps.

---

# My Reflection

Today's task emphasized customer-centric product development. Rather than building features based on assumptions, the generated blueprint focused on solving real customer problems, prioritizing an MVP, and creating a structured roadmap for launching the startup.
