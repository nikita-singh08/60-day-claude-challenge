# Day 21 – Digital Privacy Dashboard

## Objective

Learn how to use AI to generate an interactive Digital Privacy Dashboard that analyzes digital footprint, privacy risks, company exposure, data value, and provides recommendations to improve online privacy and security.

---

# Task Performed

Today I used Claude to generate a Digital Privacy Dashboard.

The workflow included:

1. Starting a new conversation in Claude.
2. Using the provided Digital Privacy Dashboard prompt.
3. Reviewing the sample dataset.
4. Generating the complete interactive HTML dashboard.
5. Reviewing my Digital Footprint Score.
6. Analyzing the Privacy Score.
7. Exploring the Exposure Heatmap.
8. Reviewing the Company Exposure Ranking.
9. Analyzing the Digital Twin Profile.
10. Reviewing the Risk Radar.
11. Studying the Most Valuable Data Assets section.
12. Reviewing the Privacy Improvement Simulator.
13. Analyzing the Final Verdict.
14. Saving the generated HTML dashboard.
15. Opening the dashboard in a browser and testing interactions.
16. Taking screenshots of important sections.

---

# Tools Used

# Dashboard Features

The generated dashboard included:

- Digital Twin Profile

- Most Valuable Data Assets
- Privacy Improvement Simulator
- Final Privacy Verdict

---

# Analysis Findings

The dashboard helped analyze:

- Overall digital footprint.
- Privacy exposure across online services.
- Personal data risk level.
- Company-wise data exposure.
- Valuable digital assets.
- Potential privacy improvements.
- Overall privacy health and recommendations.

---

# Key Learnings

- AI can generate professional interactive dashboards using sample datasets.
- Visual dashboards make privacy risks easier to understand.
- Digital footprint analysis helps identify areas that need better security.
- Interactive reports improve decision-making and awareness.
- AI can simplify complex privacy and security concepts.

---

# My Reflection

Today's activity demonstrated how AI can transform structured privacy-related data into an interactive dashboard with meaningful insights. The dashboard effectively summarized privacy risks, digital exposure, and improvement recommendations, making it easier to understand and manage digital security.
