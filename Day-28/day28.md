# Day 28 – Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator

## Objective

Build and test an AI-powered Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator to evaluate patient admission readiness by analyzing provider information, diagnosis, prior authorization status, documentation, insurance, clinical risks, and governance metrics.

---

# Task Performed

Today I used Claude AI to generate an interactive Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator.

The workflow included:

1. Starting a new conversation in Claude.
2. Using the provided Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator prompt.
3. Generating the complete HTML application.
4. Saving the generated HTML file.
5. Opening the simulator in a web browser.
6. Entering provider and physician details.
7. Selecting a diagnosis and admission type.
8. Configuring Prior Authorization status and admission date.
9. Reviewing the initial Admission Readiness Score.
10. Completing workflow actions to improve readiness.
11. Testing Prior Authorization scenarios including approval, pending requests, and appeals.
12. Reducing documentation, insurance, bed, and clinical risks.
13. Reviewing the Governance Snapshot.
14. Analyzing the final admission decision.
15. Restarting the simulator with different diagnosis scenarios.
16. Taking screenshots of the simulator and results.

# Key Learnings

- Hospital admissions depend on clinical readiness, documentation, insurance verification, and prior authorization.
- Readiness scores help identify workflow gaps before admission.
- Completing missing documentation and approvals improves admission success.
- Governance dashboards provide visibility into operational risks.
- AI-powered simulations can simplify complex healthcare workflows for learning purposes.

---

# My Reflection

Today's project demonstrated how AI can simulate hospital admission workflows and readiness assessments. The simulator provided a practical understanding of how documentation, insurance, clinical risks, and prior authorization influence admission decisions while offering an interactive educational experience.
