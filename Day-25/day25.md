# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Objective

Build and test an AI-powered Shark Tank Simulator that evaluates startup ideas, asks investor questions, provides funding recommendations, and generates a startup scorecard.

---

# Task Performed

Today I used Claude AI to generate an interactive AI Shark Tank Simulator.

The workflow included:

1. Starting a new conversation in Claude.
2. Using the provided AI Shark Tank Simulator prompt.
3. Generating the complete HTML application.
4. Saving the generated HTML file.
5. Opening the application in a browser.
6. Entering startup information.
7. Completing all startup input fields.
8. Running the Shark Tank simulation.
9. Reviewing questions from multiple AI investors.
10. Answering investor questions.
11. Analyzing the startup scorecard.
12. Reviewing the investment decision.
13. Studying valuation and funding recommendations.
14. Testing leaderboard functionality.
15. Downloading the generated pitch report.
16. Taking screenshots of the simulation and results.

# Key Learnings

- AI can simulate investor-style questioning to evaluate startup ideas.
- Strong business fundamentals improve funding recommendations.
- Investor feedback highlights potential weaknesses before real pitching.
- Interactive HTML applications can provide realistic business simulations.
- Structured startup evaluation improves product and business planning.

---

# My Reflection

Today's task demonstrated how AI can simulate a startup investment process similar to Shark Tank. The application provided valuable feedback on startup viability, valuation, funding potential, and investor concerns, making it a useful practice tool for entrepreneurs preparing investment pitches.
