# Day 19 – Football Intelligence Hub

## Objective

Learn how to use AI to analyze football knowledge, evaluate football awareness, generate FIFA World Cup 2026 predictions, assess football personality, and create a personalized Football Intelligence Profile using the provided workbook.

---

# Task Performed

Today I used Claude to generate a Football Intelligence Hub report.

The workflow included:

1. Downloading the provided football workbook.
2. Uploading the workbook into Claude.
3. Using the provided Football Intelligence Hub prompt.
4. Completing the Football Knowledge Level Check.
5. Reviewing the FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Report.
6. Completing the Football IQ Quiz.
7. Reviewing the Football Awareness Score.
8. Completing the Messi vs Ronaldo Personality Match assessment.
9. Reviewing the Football Personality Archetype.
10. Generating the final Football Intelligence Profile.
11. Saving the generated results.
12. Capturing screenshots of important outputs.
